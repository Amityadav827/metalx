About Us

An architectural metal works studio defined by precision and craft.

Our Philosophy

Metal X is a new-generation luxury architectural brand built on over 35 years of experience in the metal fabrication industry. Founded with a vision to bring our manufacturing expertise into the luxury segment, we specialize in premium balusters, staircase solutions and bespoke facade products.

As a second-generation business, Metal X combines decades of hands-on fabrication expertise with contemporary design, advanced manufacturing and a fresh global outlook. While the brand is new, it stands on a long-established foundation of craftsmanship, manufacturing knowledge and industry experience.

We are building Metal X every day with a focus on design, quality and creating products for exceptional spaces.

35+ Years of Experience. A New Vision for Luxury.

Our Facility

Manufacturing Setup

Backed by more than 35 years of metal fabrication experience, our manufacturing approach combines hands-on craftsmanship with modern production methods and precision finishing. Every component is developed with close attention to proportion, detailing and finish, supporting Metal X's range of premium balusters, staircase solutions and bespoke architectural products.

Our focus is simple: translate design intent into finely crafted metalwork suitable for exceptional residential and architectural spaces.

What We Stand For

Brand Values

01

Design with Purpose

At Metal X, design begins with understanding how metal should perform within a space. We combine contemporary aesthetics with decades of fabrication knowledge to create balusters, staircase solutions and architectural metalwork that feel refined, considered and built for lasting impact.

02

Crafted Through Experience

More than 35 years of hands-on metal fabrication experience forms the foundation behind Metal X. As a second-generation business, we bring together proven craftsmanship, manufacturing knowledge and a new-generation approach to luxury architectural products.

Quality Without Compromise

From proportion and fabrication to finishing and final detailing, quality guides every stage of our work. Our focus is on creating premium metal products with the precision, consistency and finish required for exceptional residential and architectural spaces.
