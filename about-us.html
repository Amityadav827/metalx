About Us

An architectural metal works studio defined by precision and craft.

Our Philosophy

MetalX is an architectural metal works studio that brings together precision engineering and design sensibility to create facade systems and railings of lasting distinction. We believe that every surface, every edge, every joint is an opportunity to express craft.

Our Facility

Manufacturing Setup

Our production facility houses state-of-the-art CNC machinery, laser cutting equipment, and precision welding stations. Every component passes through a rigorous quality inspection before leaving the floor.

With the capacity to handle large-scale commercial projects alongside bespoke residential commissions, our manufacturing setup is built for both scale and nuance.
