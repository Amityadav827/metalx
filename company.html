Who We Are

Built to Last

MetalX has grown from a specialist railing fabricator into a full-service architectural metal works studio. We serve architects, developers, interior designers, and contractors across India from our Sarurpur Industrial Area manufacturing facility.

Our team of 80+ engineers, fabricators, and installation specialists brings the same rigour to a single residential staircase as to a multi-storey commercial facade project.

The Workshop

Our 30,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility in Sarurpur Industrial Area houses CNC plasma cutters, laser cutting machines, precision welding bays, powder coating booths, and a dedicated quality inspection zone.

Every product is manufactured in-house — from raw material to finished installation — ensuring we maintain absolute control over quality at every step.
